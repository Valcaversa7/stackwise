## The short version

Some links on {{ site.name }} are affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. **Affiliate relationships never change what we write.** Prices you pay are identical whether you use our link or go direct.

## Which programmes we participate in

- Amazon Associates
- Individual manufacturer affiliate programmes for hardware we review
- Selected software referral programmes

This list changes over time. If a programme is not disclosed here, we are not participating in it.

## How we label them

Affiliate links are marked in the article, either inline ("affiliate link") or with a note at the top of the piece. We do not disguise them as ordinary links, and we do not use link shorteners that hide the destination.

## The rules we apply

1. **The verdict comes first.** We write the review, reach a conclusion, and only then decide whether to include a purchase link. A product we do not recommend does not get an affiliate link.
2. **No payment for coverage.** We do not accept payment in exchange for publishing a review, and we do not accept payment for a positive one. Review units are disclosed in the article.
3. **No advance review.** Manufacturers do not see articles before publication and cannot request changes.
4. **Commission rates do not rank products.** We do not sort recommendations by what pays best. If we ever find ourselves doing that, the article is wrong and we will fix it.
5. **We disclose review units.** If a manufacturer sent us the hardware, the article says so at the top.

## FTC compliance

In line with the US Federal Trade Commission's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (16 CFR Part 255), we disclose the existence of affiliate relationships on the pages where they appear, not only on this page.
