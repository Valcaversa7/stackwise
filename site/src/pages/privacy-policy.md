This Privacy Policy explains what information {{ site.name }} ("we", "us") collects when you visit [stackwise.dpdns.org]({{ site.url }}), why we collect it, and the choices you have. It applies to all pages on this website.

If you have questions after reading this, contact us at [stackwiseorg@gmail.com](mailto:stackwiseorg@gmail.com) and we will respond within 30 days.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us

We only collect personal information when you choose to provide it:

- **Newsletter subscriptions.** When you subscribe, we collect your email address and the date you subscribed. We use it for one purpose: sending the newsletter you asked for.
- **Contact messages.** When you use the contact form, we collect your name, email address and the content of your message, so that we can reply.
- **Comments.** If a commenting system is enabled, we collect the name, email address and content you submit, along with your IP address and browser user agent for spam prevention.

We do not require an account to read anything on this site, and we never sell your personal information.

### Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we collect a limited amount of technical information automatically:

- Your IP address (truncated before storage where our analytics provider supports it)
- Browser type, operating system and device type
- Referring page and pages viewed
- Approximate geographic region derived from your IP address
- Date and time of each request

We use this in aggregate to understand which articles are useful and to detect abuse such as scraping or credential-stuffing attempts.

### Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage for three purposes: remembering your cookie-consent choice, remembering your theme preference, and — with your consent — measuring readership and serving advertising. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookie-policy/) for the complete list.

## 2. Advertising and Google AdSense

**This section is the one you are probably here for.**

We display advertising on this site through **Google AdSense**, a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC. When you visit a page containing an advertisement, Google and its partners may store or retrieve information on your browser, usually in the form of cookies, and process personal data such as your IP address, approximate location, and browsing behaviour on this and other websites.

### What Google and its partners use this for

- **Serving personalised ads.** Ads based on your previous visits to this website and other websites on the internet.
- **Serving non-personalised ads.** Ads based on the content of the page you are reading and your general location, rather than your interests.
- **Measuring ad performance,** including how often an ad is shown, whether you clicked it, and whether it led to a purchase or sign-up.
- **Frequency capping and fraud prevention.**

### TheDoubleClick / Google advertising cookie

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site. Google's use of the **DoubleClick cookie** enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. These cookies are used to:

- serve ads based on a user's previous visits to this or other websites
- measure how effective an ad campaign was
- avoid showing you the same ad too many times

### Third-party vendors and ad servers

Google is not the only company involved. Third-party vendors and ad networks — including, potentially, Criteo, Taboola, OpenX, Index Exchange, PubMatic and AppNexus — also use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to serve ads on this site. Each of these vendors has its own privacy policy governing its use of your data.

A current list of the Google-certified ad technology providers that may serve ads on this site is available in the [Google Ad Technology Provider list](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/9490902). We have not individually selected every vendor on that list; Google manages the auction.

### Your choices

You have several ways to control this, and we would genuinely encourage you to use them:

1. **Refuse or withdraw consent on this site.** Our cookie banner lets you decline personalisation. If you decline, you will still see advertisements — they will simply be less relevant to you.
2. **Opt out of personalised advertising from Google** at [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads).
3. **Opt out of personalised advertising from many third-party vendors at once** at [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/) (US) or [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/) (EU).
4. **Block advertising cookies in your browser.** Most browsers let you block third-party cookies entirely.
5. **Use an ad blocker.** We will not detect this, punish you for it, or show you a wall.

Google's own explanation of how its advertising cookies work is at [policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

### What we do not do

We do not share your email address, name or contact details with any advertising partner. We do not use sensitive-interest categories (health, religion, political opinion, sexual orientation) for advertising. We do not knowingly serve personalised advertising to visitors we believe to be under 16.

## 3. Analytics

We do not build individual user profiles from analytics data.

If we ever change analytics provider, this section will be updated before the change takes effect.

## 6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate data
- **Delete** your data
- **Restrict or object to** processing
- **Export** your data in a portable format
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting prior processing

For most readers this is simple: unsubscribing from the newsletter deletes your email address within 30 days, and we hold very little else. To exercise any other right, email [stackwiseorg@gmail.com](mailto:stackwiseorg@gmail.com). We will respond within 30 days.

**EEA, UK and Swiss readers:** you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

**California readers:** under the CCPA/CPRA you have the right to know, delete and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information. We do not sell personal information. Advertising that involves sharing identifiers with ad partners may constitute "sharing" under California law; use the cookie banner to opt out.

## 7. Children

This site is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
