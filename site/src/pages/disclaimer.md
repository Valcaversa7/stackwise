Everything published on Stackwise is provided for general informational and educational purposes. It is not professional advice — not legal, financial, medical, security or engineering advice — and it does not create a professional relationship between you and us.

Technology changes quickly. An article that was accurate when published may become outdated. We update articles where we can and date every piece, but you should verify anything critical against primary sources before relying on it.

## Not a substitute for professional advice

Decisions with significant financial, legal or safety consequences — buying a business system, restructuring infrastructure, handling regulated data — should involve a qualified professional who understands your specific situation. We cannot provide that, and nothing on this site should be read as if we did.

## Accuracy and testing

We test what we review, and we describe our methods in the article. But our testing has limits you should know about:

- Hardware results depend on the specific unit we received, its firmware version and our environment. Your unit may differ.
- Software results depend on the version tested. Updates change behaviour.
- Benchmarks measure a workload we chose. They may not represent yours.

Where a result surprised us or contradicted the manufacturer's claim, we say so and show the numbers. Where we did not test something, we say that too.
