{{ site.name }} publishes guides, tutorials and reviews about the tools people actually work with — version control, editors, shells, hardware, and the small systems that decide whether a working day goes smoothly.

It is run by three people who write about this stuff because we could not find the articles we wanted to read.

## What we write

Three sections, and the distinction matters:

- **[Guides](/guides/)** — opinionated, long-form writing about choosing and configuring tools. A guide takes a position and defends it.
- **[Tutorials](/tutorials/)** — step-by-step instructions with a working end state. If a step can fail, we say what the failure looks like.
- **[Reviews](/reviews/)** — hardware and software assessed with measurements where measurement is possible, and with the method published.

We do not write news. We do not write listicles assembled from other people's listicles. If an article cannot survive the question "would you send this to a colleague", it does not get published.

## How we test

For hardware, that means instruments: a power meter, a sound level meter, a colour probe, a stopwatch, and the same workload run on every device so the numbers mean something. For software, that means a week of real use rather than a weekend, on a real project rather than a demo.

We publish the method in the article. If you think we measured the wrong thing, that is a legitimate criticism and we want to hear it.

Our full standards, including how we handle corrections and commercial relationships, are in the [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/).

## How the site is funded

Two ways, both disclosed:

1. **Display advertising**, served by Google AdSense. Ads appear in labelled spaces and never interrupt the reading column. You can block them; we will not show you a wall for doing so.
2. **Affiliate links** on some product recommendations. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. See the [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/).

We do not accept payment for coverage, we do not accept sponsored posts, and advertisers never see an article before it is published.
