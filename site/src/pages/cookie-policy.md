This policy lists the cookies and similar technologies used on Stackwise. It sits alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/), which explains advertising and data handling in more detail.

## What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. Cookies are not programs and cannot read your hard disk. They let a site remember something about you — that you dismissed a banner, that you chose a dark theme, or that you have seen an advertisement before.

## Cookies we set ourselves

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sw-consent` | Remembers your cookie-consent choice so we do not ask again | 12 months |
| `sw-theme` (localStorage) | Remembers a manually chosen light/dark theme | Until cleared |

Both are strictly necessary for the site to function as you configured it. Neither tracks you across other websites.

## Analytics (no cookies)

We use **Vercel Web Analytics** (operated by Vercel Inc.) to measure aggregate readership — page views, referrers and approximate region. It sets no cookies and uses no persistent identifiers or cross-site tracking, so it does not appear in the tables above. More information: [vercel.com/docs/analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics).

## Cookies set by our advertising partners

When you view a page containing an advertisement, Google AdSense and its partners may set cookies. These are **third-party cookies** — set by a domain other than ours.

| Provider | Purpose | Documentation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google (DoubleClick) | Serving and measuring personalised ads | [policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) |
| Certified third-party ad vendors | Ad auction participation, frequency capping | [Google ad tech provider list](https://support.google.com/admanager/answer/9490902) |

These cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising at [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads), or across many vendors at [youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu/) and [aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## Your consent

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, we show a consent banner before setting or reading advertising cookies. If you decline, you will still see advertisements — they will be non-personalised. Your choice is stored for 12 months and you can change it at any time by clearing the `sw-consent` cookie or using the link in our footer.

We do not sell your consent state to anyone.

## Controlling cookies yourself

Every modern browser lets you inspect and delete cookies:

- **Chrome / Edge / Brave:** Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
- **Firefox:** Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
- **Safari:** Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data

Blocking third-party cookies entirely will stop advertising personalisation on this and every other site. Blocking all cookies will mean our consent banner reappears on every visit and your theme preference will not be remembered.

## Do Not Track

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no industry-standard response to it, so we handle the underlying preference through the consent banner instead, which gives you an explicit and enforceable choice.
